Publication

Preprint · bioRxiv · 2024 · Biophysics

Computational modelling of specific serotonin reuptake inhibitor inhibition on the TREK-2 channel

Narawit Pratueangsukpong, Tanadet Pipatpolkai

Using molecular dynamics simulations, we showed that the antidepressants fluoxetine and escitalopram bind the same pocket on TREK-2, a mechanosensitive potassium channel, yet inhibit it in different ways. Fluoxetine acts through the surrounding POPC lipid and disrupts gating at the selectivity filter, while escitalopram behaves as a conventional pore blocker.
